Controls
  • Left thumb: drag anywhere on the left to fly (the stick floats under your thumb). Desktop: WASD / arrows
  • GUN (hold): chain gun · MSL: Hellfires, homing once the reticle turns red · MINE: drop an explosive mine
  • Desktop: Space / J gun · K missile · L mine · E exit / enter · C camera · P pause · M mute
  • Gamepad: left stick fly, right stick aim the nose, RT gun, RB/A missile, LB/B mine, X exit / enter, Y camera
  • Holding fire swings the nose onto the nearest target. Hover on the LZ pad to rearm and refuel; fly over crates to winch them up.
  • STRIKE camera: the view sits behind you and the stick turns the nose (Soviet Strike controls).
